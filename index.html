How a report becomes trusted intelligence

Automation moves fast, but nothing reaches the public map until a person has checked it. This is what keeps Veriscope an intelligence source rather than a rumour feed.

Collect

Reports are gathered continuously from vetted news outlets, official statements and moderated public submissions.

Extract

Natural language processing pulls the event type, date, location, actors and impact from raw text into a structured record.

Verify

A human reviewer confirms the facts, requires a second source for social claims, and sets the confidence level.

Publish

Only confirmed incidents are mapped, at a location precision that never overstates what the source actually supports.

Trust is the product

Every published record carries the marks of how it was checked.
